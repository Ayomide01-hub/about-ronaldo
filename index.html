Welcome Message:
Welcome to the journey of one of football's greatest legends — Cristiano Ronaldo. From the streets of Madeira to the world stage, Ronaldo's passion, hard work, and hunger for greatness have made him a global icon.

Bio Data

  • Name: Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro
  • Date of Birth: February 5, 1985
  • Nationality: Portuguese
  • Height: 1.87 m (6 ft 2 in)
  • Weight: Approx. 83 kg
  • Position: Forward
  • Preferred Foot: Right

Early Life & Background

Cristiano was born into a humble family as the youngest of four children. His mother, Maria Dolores, was a cook, and his father, José Dinis Aveiro, was a gardener and kitman. Despite challenges like poverty and his father's struggles with alcohol, Ronaldo's love for football was clear from a young age.
